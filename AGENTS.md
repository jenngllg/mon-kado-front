# MonKado frontend instructions

- Notification preference qualifiers belong inline in parentheses within each label, without a separate helper line. Use “Rappels d’évènements (J-7 et J-1)” for occasion reminders.

- Public and group-scoped member profile pages use a 112px avatar, double the former 56px size, on mobile and desktop.

Read and apply [the shared MonKado instructions](../mon-kado/AGENTS.shared.md), which supplement Jenn's global preferences in `~/.codex/AGENTS.md`. This file adds frontend-specific rules.

- Read `DESIGN_SYSTEM.md` before creating or changing any interface. Apply it to existing screens and new features; user-approved refinements take precedence over older mockups.
- Reuse the existing tokens and shared controls. Match the original mockup layouts and illustrated cards; do not simplify or reinterpret them without an explicit request. Keep the user's requested page actions at the top right and never underline navigation, buttons, tabs or action links in any state.
- Keep real prose links distinguishable, preserve keyboard focus and accessible names.
- Group member cards use four equal-width columns on desktop, two at tablet widths and one on mobile. Keep long names wrapping and preserve the avatar, profile navigation and accessible removal control.
- UX refinements approved by Jenn on 1 November 2026: mobile list overviews use compact horizontal occasion thumbnails with immediately visible titles. Public/group reading banners stay compact on mobile and use headings sized for their available width; messages on sage use forest-green text.
- Keep creation steps close to their fields, omit empty status layout slots, and show a labelled image-add button before an image is selected. Account settings use two navigation columns on mobile. Preserve existing images, keyboard access and drafts.
- Wish creation uses an exclusive native radio choice “Un produit précis / Surprends-moi”, numbered steps and a visible step count on mobile. Keep “Précédent” on the left, “Continuer / Ajouter le souhait” on the right and a visibly labelled return to the list. Mobile actions stick within the form while retaining their normal layout space; let them scroll normally during text entry and on short screens so they never cover the focused field.
- The optional product link comes first on wish-creation step 2 for “Un produit précis”. Step 1 only chooses the type and always uses “Continuer”. Preserve the link and the rest of the draft on return or mode changes; validate the visible product fields on step 2.
- Followed lists offer member search as their contextual action; empty reservation history offers access to groups. Eligible group readers get a single “Proposer une idée” action beside the “Idées surprises proposées” section heading, with the same private group scope. Do not repeat the same action on the same page; keep contextual actions with their corresponding section.
- Owner list details show a concise audience summary read from the server. Always use “Partagée avec X groupes” (singular for one) for positive group counts, independently of public sharing; show “Lien public actif / Lien public inactif” separately. Refresh it after sharing dialogs close; never infer “Seulement moi” after a failed read. Creation explicitly labels an empty group selection “Seulement moi”.
- Use the tutoiement consistently and distinguish missing required fields from invalid values. Reuse the horizontal wish-quantity stepper in suggestion dialogs. The mobile dashboard offers direct list/group shortcuts before upcoming occasions.
- Do not add plant, leaf or botanical drawings as site decoration, including page backgrounds, list overviews and shared/group views. Preserve occasion-cover photographs and member-provided images.
- Notifications use an accessible header bell and a live unread dot. Keep the bell beside Compte on desktop, and outside the collapsed menu between search and Menu on phones/tablets. Their compact panel and search field align under their triggers and stay inside header gutters. Notification updates must not refresh unrelated business views.
- On mobile/tablet, put Paramètres and Se déconnecter directly in the main menu, without an avatar or nested account menu. Keep equal gaps between search, notifications and Menu, wrapping those controls together below the logo when required. Desktop retains the avatar-labelled Compte disclosure.
- Dashboard list covers fill their cards' height and meet the left/top/bottom edges with rounded outer corners. On tablets, place the upcoming-occasions heading above equal columns, with dividers only between occasions and the first occasion aligned with the heading.

- Keep interface copy concise. Avoid redundant explanations and obvious field instructions; do not add helper text by default.
- Jenn's refresh policy, agreed on 2 November 2026: load business data on page entry and after explicit actions on that page. Do not refresh business data on focus, Alt+Tab, visibility changes, a timer, polling or a live subscription. Changes made elsewhere are shown after manual reload or a new page entry. Preserve session/security synchronization and fresh authorization/version checks required by explicit actions.
- Successful sign-in opens the authenticated home dashboard (`/`), including email, Google, two-factor completion and cross-tab sign-in. Preserve the existing extension-add continuation and authentication safeguards.
- The extension's multi-list section opens expanded by default and stays expanded when editing other fields. Keep a fixed bold “Listes *” heading with its disclosure arrow, without a border, background panel or selected names. Show selected list names only among the expanded checkboxes; retain manual collapse and keyboard access.
- Extension surprise ideas start with a person chosen from the current member's groups, followed by multiple selectable shared-list destinations. Label each destination with its group, keep separate group/list privacy scopes and submit directly without requiring an open web editor. Keep personal and surprise drafts separate, preserve confirmed additions during partial failures and block replay of uncertain results.
- Preserve useful validation errors, accessibility labels and necessary legal information. Show constraints when they help resolve an error rather than repeating them under every field.
- Mark required fields with an asterisk; do not append “facultatif” to optional field labels.
- Call wishlist entries “souhaits” in interface labels, actions and feedback; reserve “cadeaux” for general gift-related marketing copy.

- Browser tabs for wishlist and wish detail/edit pages use the loaded resource name followed by ` · MonKado`, including shared views; keep titles current after saved renames and prevent stale responses from changing another page's title.

- Editable list pages place the return arrow first, the centered list title on the plain page below it, then a sage banner with occasion/date/countdown on the left and the optional description on the right. These two sections each occupy 50% of the desktop banner, with the divider in the middle. Center the content horizontally and vertically within each section. Below the banner, align sorting to the left and list actions to the right, preserving their current order. Add 0.75rem of breathing room above and below these toolbar controls. Sorting comes first in keyboard order; keep each side aligned when wrapping on narrow screens, and stack metadata before the description. Apply this to personal lists opened from groups as well.

- Local toolbar comparison requested by Jenn: keep sorting left; show secondary “Partager”, the “⋯” list-options disclosure, then the primary labelled “Ajouter un souhait” on the right. Put edit, reorder, archive/restore and delete in that disclosure; retain existing permissions, confirmations and focus recovery. Group audience and public sharing live together under “Partager”, in independent “Mes groupes / Lien public” tabs.
- Workflow refinements approved by Jenn on 2 November 2026: group member profiles show shared lists without a “Proposer une idée” action. Proposals start only in a selected list’s ideas section. Keep group privacy, confirmed additions and uncertain-write protections. Wish details prioritize “Modifier”; group details prioritize “Inviter un proche”. Put copy/delete and group management/leave/delete in their labelled “Options” disclosures. The idea editor shows link, name, price and image, with description, quantity and optional extension help under “Précisions”; keep errors discoverable and mobile actions accessible without covering text entry.

- The home/header logo combines the existing MonKado wordmark and coral dot with the favicon gift outline in coral on its left, shifted upward by 1 px for optical alignment.
- Group suggestion cards reuse the reading wishes' grid, horizontal card proportions and contained image sizing at every breakpoint. Clicking the card or its keyboard-accessible title opens its private detail page with a fresh authorized read. Place reservation on the image using the reading wishes' control; omit product links, notes and quantity/availability counters from the card. Preserve their private scope and reservation/edit/delete controls; do not enlarge their images or switch only suggestions to vertical cards on mobile.

- Suggestion cards have no edit button. Open the card to reach editing through its private detail page. Author deletion uses the existing red trash icon in a 44px round control at the card’s top right, revealed on desktop hover or keyboard focus and always available on touch. Keep confirmation, reserved-idea restrictions and focus restoration.

- Suggestion details use a dedicated authenticated group/list/suggestion URL and the same page layout as wish details, never a detail modal. Preserve the list’s sort and member origin in navigation, fresh private reads, resource tab titles and image cleanup on leaving the page.
- Suggestion detail actions follow wish details: keep author-only “Modifier” and the labelled “Options de l’idée” disclosure in the top-right toolbar, with textual red “Supprimer” inside Options. Place the external-product icon directly beside the merchant link; no management controls in the description block.
- Dates in sage list banners use the existing terracotta text token in both owner/editable and shared/group reading views, at every breakpoint.

- Technical recovery must not add manual “Vérifier”, “Relire” or image-retry controls. Reconcile an uncertain explicit operation with a bounded, authorized read when possible, preserving drafts and never replaying its writes. Media recovery renews only the failed image; it must not refresh other business information or run on a timer, focus or visibility change. Retain meaningful business confirmations and security checks.
